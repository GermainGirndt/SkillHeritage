🛠 Tools Needed

  • 10mm Socket Wrench
  • Flathead Screwdriver
  • Safety Gloves

📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preparation: Ensure the engine is cold before starting. Disconnect negative battery terminal.
  2. Cover Removal: Locate the 3 bolts on the engine cover. Use the 10mm wrench to remove them.
  3. Extraction: The part is held by clips. Use the flathead screwdriver to gently release the pressure.
  4. Installation: Place the new part in reverse order. Tighten bolts to 15 Nm.

⚠️ Safety Notes

Be careful with the hot manifold. Do not over-tighten the plastic screws.
